## ❓ Q&A Prep for Judges

**Q: How does VetNexus 305 differ from existing military skills translators?**  
*A: Existing tools (like Military.com) provide rigid, 1-to-1 job title lookups that lead to commercial job board ads. VetNexus 305 decodes complex military duties into quantified STAR accomplishment bullets, tailors civilian impact metrics, calculates an ATS keyword score, and uniquely integrates 38 CFR disability evidence preparation.*

**Q: Why is privacy so critical for this product?**  
*A: Veterans are rightfully protective of their Service Treatment Records (STRs), mental health history, and DD-214 documents. By implementing a 100% client-side execution model, no personal health information or military records are stored in external cloud databases or harvested for ad targeting.*

**Q: How does this help South Florida and FIU specifically?**  
*A: FIU hosts over 1,200 military-affiliated students. VetNexus 305 provides direct, verified connections to the FIU VMA office (SASC 230), the Bruce W. Carter VA Medical Center in Miami, the Florida Department of Veterans' Affairs accredited officers, and emergency crisis relief.*
